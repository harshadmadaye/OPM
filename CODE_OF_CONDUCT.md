# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith. Argue about the code,
not the person writing it.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the work. "This function has a race condition" is useful;
  "you clearly did not think about this" is not.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way. A reviewer finding a bug is
  doing you a favour.
- Respect that people contribute in whatever time they have, for free.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone was one.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Demeaning comments about who someone is rather than what they wrote.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, or sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere
else someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by emailing the address on
[the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/harshadmadaye). Reports are
read privately and handled as quickly as is practical.

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block
accounts. Serious or repeated behaviour gets a permanent ban. A report will not
be held against the person who made it.

This is a small project with one maintainer, so expect a human response rather
than a process. The aim is that everyone can take part without having to put up
with anything.
