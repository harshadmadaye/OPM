# Design artifact outline

Order of sections (or artboards on a canvas). Every section is short; the
wireframes carry the weight.

1. Overview: one-line pitch, goals, non-goals, success signal.
2. Personas and flows: two or three personas, each with one end-to-end flow.
3. Screen inventory: every screen with a low-fi wireframe, its purpose, entry points, and empty, loading and error states.
4. Navigation map: how screens connect; tabs, stacks, deep links.
5. Data model: entities, fields that matter, relations, ownership and access.
6. Architecture: client, backend, data store, auth, third parties, where each runs.
7. API or contract list: endpoints, callable functions, or Firestore collections with read/write rules summary.
8. Stack: chosen technologies with one reason each.
9. Milestone outline: foundation, feature slices, hardening.
10. Assumptions and open questions: what the developer should challenge before approving.

Keep the artifact title stable across revisions. Log each revision at the bottom: date, what changed, why.
