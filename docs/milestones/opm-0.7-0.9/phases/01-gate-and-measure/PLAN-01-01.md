<objective>
Turn OPM's core promise, a small always-on token cost, into a measured number that CI enforces. A zero-dependency linter reports always-on tokens and per-skill tokens, fails when the always-on total passes its ceiling, and regenerates a table in docs/why-opm.md. Later phases lower the ceiling as skills are trimmed.
</objective>
