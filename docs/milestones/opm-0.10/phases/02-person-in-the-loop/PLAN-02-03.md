A 20 to 30 minute review pack at every phase close (spec item 7). A zero-dependency script ranks the phase's commits by risk (auth, permissions, data deletion, migrations, payments, sync, secrets, external calls) and prints a skeleton with the five riskiest, each with its `git show` range and files. The orchestrator fills in 2 or 3 "explain how X handles Y" questions per item from the diff and saves REVIEW-PACK.md in the phase folder. Phase 1's hand-written pack (phases/01-orchestration-reliability/REVIEW-PACK.md) is the target shape.
</objective>
